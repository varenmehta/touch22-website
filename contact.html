FAQ

Questions retailers ask us.

Who can buy from Touch22?

We supply jewellery retailers, chain stores and trade buyers in India and overseas. We don't sell directly to consumers; ask your local jeweller for Touch22 designs.

How do I see what's in stock?

Our catalogue shows designs with codes, purity and weights. Registered retailers see live ready stock, updated daily, on the touch22.in portal.

Which purities do you make?

T-22 (22KT), T-18 (18KT) and T-9 (9KT). The purity is listed against every design code in the catalogue.

How is the price worked out?

Net weight × the day's gold rate for the purity, plus making charges and any stone charges, plus GST. The catalogue shows an estimate; our sales team confirms the final price on your order.

Do you take export orders?

Yes. Choose "Export enquiry" in the form above and our export desk will get back to you with a catalogue and terms.

Can designs be made to order?

Yes. Designs marked "Made to order" in the catalogue are produced against your order; share the code and quantity and we'll confirm timelines.
