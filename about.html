Our story

Zaveri Bazaar roots.
A different gold experience.

Touch22 is the jewellery house of Unitouch Creations LLP. We design, make and supply gold jewellery to retailers, chain stores and trade buyers, so the pieces on their counters sell.

Who we are

Built for the people behind the counter.

We work business to business. Our customers are jewellers who need designs that move, weights they can trust and stock that is there when they call.

That is why our catalogue is ready stock, updated daily. A retailer can browse touch22.in, add designs to a cart and have our team confirm it the same day. Our Trending@Touch picks show what is selling across the country each month.

From a showroom in Mumbai's Zaveri Bazaar, a close team of about twelve people looks after every order and every relationship, and we are now taking Touch22 to buyers overseas.
