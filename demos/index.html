What we need from Touch22 to build it

  1. Which platform runs touch22.in, and can it send cart and order events (webhook or daily export)?
  2. Is WhatsApp Business API set up, and which number should the assistant use?
  3. Which billing or stock software do you use (for example Omunim, Gati, Jewel Soft), and can it export stock daily?
  4. Your pricing rules: making charges, wastage or touch, and stone charges by category.
  5. Who approves Instagram posts and retailer messages today, and how many posts a week you want.
  6. A folder of your original product photos (the same ones on Instagram) to load into the studio.
